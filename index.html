No-go / template collapse

The gate caught a convincing failure.

D4 Shot 1 continued from the verified v02 adapter and scored 16.35/20 on the same locked 240-example evaluation—worse than both the 19.57 champion and the 18.03 base model. This was not a reset or a pipeline mistake. The degradation came from the training itself.

Failure mode

D4 invented the phrase “available evidence is sufficient” and repeated it across 233 responses. It was not parroting the training data; it had learned the shape of a good answer without the substance—a clear template collapse.

Forensic result

The lineage audit verified that D4 loaded the exact learned v02 state and kept its adapter trainable. No wrong checkpoint, accidental reset, stacking error, or unexpected merge explained the drop.

What survived

The evaluation gate did its job: v02 stayed champion at 19.57/20 through this run—and held the title until faithful-v01's 19.59/20 full-eval result. The failed run produced a real lead for the next controlled experiment—test how context length and training dynamics affect retention without changing the incumbent.
